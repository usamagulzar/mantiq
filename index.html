Mantiq is built to make Boolean logic easier to see and easier to understand. It exists to help you check your own working, explore how simplification actually happens step by step, and build the kind of intuition that only comes from engaging with a concept directly.

It is not built to think through a problem on your behalf, and it should not be used to produce work that you submit as your own for a graded assignment, quiz, or exam. Typing in a problem and copying out the answer skips the part where the actual learning happens.

By continuing, you agree to use Mantiq as a study aid rather than a substitute for the effort your coursework is meant to require of you.

About Mantiq

What it is, and how to get the most out of it

Mantiq

Turn Your Logic Into Life

Mantiq is a free, offline-capable digital logic workbench for students, educators, and engineers. Type a Boolean expression and instantly get a minimized solution, a step-by-step algebraic proof, a Karnaugh map, a truth table, an auto-generated circuit diagram you can simulate live, and clean exportable Verilog - all powered by a compiled WebAssembly logic engine, right in your browser.

Version 2.0.0 Works Fully Offline WebAssembly Engine Free & Open Source
What You Can Do

Minimize Expressions

Simplify any Boolean expression to its minimal SOP or POS form using the Quine-McCluskey method.

Algebraic Proofs

See every law - absorption, distribution, De Morgan's, and more - applied step by step.

Karnaugh Maps

Auto-grouped, color-coded K-Maps for 2-6 variables with prime implicant highlighting.

Truth Tables

Full, instantly generated truth tables with minterm and maxterm callouts.

Live Circuit Simulation

An auto-drawn gate diagram you can interact with - flip inputs and watch outputs respond in real time.

Verilog Export

Generate clean, ready-to-use Verilog HDL from your simplified expression in one click.

Built for the classroom and beyond. Whether you're checking Digital Logic Design homework, prepping for an exam, teaching K-Maps, or prototyping a circuit for fun, Mantiq turns abstract Boolean algebra into something you can see, simplify, and simulate.

Why Mantiq?

How Mantiq compares to other tools

Each of these tools serves a different purpose. Here is an honest look at where they overlap and where they differ.

Feature Mantiq Wolfram|Alpha Logisim-evolution
Boolean SimplificationSOP & POSSOP & POSSOP & POS
K-Map2–6 variablesNoUp to 4 variables
Step-by-step algebraic proofYesNoNo
Quine-McCluskeyShown step by stepNoRuns internally, not shown
Truth tableYesYesYes
Circuit diagramAuto-generatedStatic diagramFull schematic editor
Interactive simulationIn browserNoDesktop app
Verilog exportGate & dataflowNoVia FPGA synthesis tools
Runs offlinePWA, no installNoDesktop install
Free to useYesFree tier, paid ProOpen source

Wolfram|Alpha is a general-purpose math engine — it simplifies Boolean expressions and generates truth tables and circuit diagrams, but is not focused on digital logic education. Logisim-evolution is a full-featured desktop circuit simulator built for designing and testing complete circuits, with Boolean analysis as one component. Mantiq is narrower in scope than Logisim and less general than Wolfram|Alpha, but is purpose-built around the Boolean algebra workflow a DLD course actually requires.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

1. No Personal Data Collection

Mantiq does not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. No account is required. No form submissions are involved. Your Boolean expressions and logic inputs exist only in your browser's memory and are never sent to any server.

2. Local Storage

Mantiq stores a small amount of data in your browser's local storage to remember your theme preference (light or dark mode) and whether you have acknowledged the academic integrity notice. This data never leaves your device.

Mantiq uses Google Analytics (GA4) to collect anonymous, aggregated usage statistics, such as page views and general geographic region. No personally identifiable information is collected. You can opt out via your browser's privacy settings or a standard ad-blocker extension.
